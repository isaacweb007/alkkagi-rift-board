# Alkkagi Design Review Checklist

## Core fun

- Can a new player understand select-drag-release in under one minute?
- Can an expert express contact point, power, spin, bank shots, and positional setup?
- Does every rule remain readable from the main camera?
- Is a standard match targeted at 5–8 minutes with a hard upper bound?

## Fairness

- Are both players subject to identical physics and turn timing?
- Is ranked competitive power independent of spending?
- Are aim assistance and prediction identical across devices and input methods?
- Can every legal result be reconstructed from the authoritative match log?

## Content

- Does every story beat introduce, test, or combine a mechanic?
- Does each arena change atmosphere before it changes competitive geometry?
- Does every character have recognizable silhouette, role, counterplay, and sound cue?
- Are launch characters made from a reusable modular production kit?

## Economy and safety

- Is the fun loop playable without a wallet?
- Are closed-loop points separated from withdrawable assets in UI, ledger, and language?
- Are minors, restricted regions, spending limits, fraud, disputes, and self-exclusion addressed?
- Has legal review approved every deposit, reward, and withdrawal flow before launch?

## Scope

- Does the MVP prove physics, input, camera, multiplayer authority, reconnect, and replay?
- Are token withdrawal, user trading, and many characters deferred until retention is proven?
- Are performance budgets defined for the PC web target?
